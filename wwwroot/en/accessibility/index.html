Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

At Engel Real Estate we invest as much as possible in providing all our clients with equal and accessible service, to allow a comfortable browsing experience for the entire population, including people with disabilities, in accordance with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law.

Accessibility adjustments were made on this site in accordance with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 2013, as strictly as possible.

The adjustments follow the recommendations of Israeli Standard 5568 for web content accessibility at level AA and the international WCAG 2.0 document.

Site accessibility

The site includes an accessibility menu, opened with the accessibility button at the bottom of the screen. The menu includes:

  • Increase and decrease text size
  • Grayscale
  • High contrast and inverted contrast
  • Light background
  • Highlight links
  • Readable font
  • Reset settings

In addition, the site is built with a proper heading structure, full keyboard navigation, alternative text for images, support for the Reduced Motion preference and ARIA attributes in menus and forms.

Accessibility enquiries

We continue to work on improving the site's accessibility as part of our commitment to providing equal and fair service to everyone. If you encounter any accessibility problem, please let us know and we will make every effort to find a suitable solution and resolve it as soon as possible.
