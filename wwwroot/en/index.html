03 Urban renewal

Two tracks. One standard.

The full process

TAMA 38/2

Demolition & rebuild

A completely new building, from the parking basements to the top roof - luxurious design, rich specification and a high construction standard.

  • New, functional, light-filled apartments at the maximum area allowed by the Tel Aviv municipality's rights
  • Green building, acoustic insulation and a grand lobby with a high-spec elevator
  • A functional parking garage for all apartments - conventional or robotic
  • A personal design meeting with an architect for every resident

TAMA 38/1

Strengthening & renovation

Seismic strengthening of the building to the strictest standards, together with a full upgrade of the building and its apartments.

  • Larger apartments: sun balconies, safe rooms and additional areas
  • A new elevator and an upgraded lobby and stairwell
  • High-finish exterior renovation and new infrastructure: sewage, electricity, water and gas
  • Access paths and a designed garden with advanced irrigation
  1. Feasibility check

    An initial check that the building meets the plan's conditions - at no cost.

  2. Due diligence & planning

    Legal checks with the authorities, engineering and architectural design, and appointing the consultant team.

  3. Resident agreements

    Guiding the residents and finalising the project details, specification and finish level.

  4. Financing & construction

    Bank financing, sale-law guarantees, temporary housing financing - and closely supervised construction.

  5. Handover & registration

    Delivering the new apartments, regularising the bylaws and registering in the Land Registry.
